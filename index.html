About Sahaj Tathya Mitra Kendra

Founded in 2024, Sahaj Tathya Mitra Kendra has been serving the community with reliable internet and digital services. We understand the importance of connectivity in today's world and strive to provide affordable, high-quality services to all our customers.

Our mission is to bridge the digital divide by providing access to technology and digital services to everyone, regardless of their technical expertise.

Our team consists of friendly, knowledgeable staff who are always ready to assist you with any of your digital needs.
